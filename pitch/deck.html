The hard part nobody solves06 / 18

Escrow is a regulated activity,
not a feature.

Holding third-party funds falls under the Bank of Ghana — Payment Systems and Services Act, 2019 (Act 987).

Attempt Nº1 — Ignore it
Run unlicensed custody until shut down.
Attempt Nº2 — Wait for a licence
Millions of GHS in capital. Years of approval.
Croe never holds funds on its own account. A licensed entity always holds the float.

Appendix · A2A2

Technical depth.

MoneyNUMERIC(15,2) throughout; no floats anywhere near a balance. Explicit currency on every amount.
LedgerAppend-only; UPDATE/DELETE revoked at the database role level.
ConcurrencySELECT … FOR UPDATE before every money mutation; partial unique indexes make double-deposit structurally impossible.
WebhooksRaw-body HMAC verification, 300-second replay window, Redis fast-gate plus durable inbox.
Ordering rulePay first, then ledger — the negative entry written only after provider confirmation.
ReconciliationDaily three-way match. Any mismatch freezes disbursements and pages.
