## How Croe Works (In 4 Simple Steps)

```
   1. LINK               2. SECURED             3. DISPATCH             4. CASHOUT
Create a link       Buyer pays via MoMo    Send your rider        Buyer confirms delivery
in 10 seconds       Money is locked safe   with 100% peace        MoMo hits your wallet
& send in chat      You can see it         of mind                automatically
```

1. **Generate a Payment Link:** In the Croe app, type the item name and amount (e.g. *iPhone 12 - GHS 2,400*). Paste the link into your WhatsApp or Instagram DM.
2. **Buyer Pays via Normal MoMo:** The buyer taps the link and authorizes payment using their standard MoMo USSD prompt (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, or AirtelTigo).
3. **Money Is Locked in Escrow:** Both you and the buyer instantly see the funds are secured. You know with 100% certainty the money is real and locked. The buyer knows you cannot touch it until they receive their package.
4. **Ship & Get Paid:** Dispatch the item with your regular dispatch rider. When the buyer confirms delivery, the money automatically lands in your MoMo wallet.
