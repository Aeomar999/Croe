The circular mark for the moments money is locked or returned — rotated −8°, as if stamped by hand. Cobalt by default; done-green only on resolution screens. One seal per screen, never more.

Ochre means ‘being sorted out’, never ‘you’re in trouble’.

Caution in Croe is warm — a state that resolves, not a fault to fear. There is no red stamp, and there never will be.
